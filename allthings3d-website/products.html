Signature

Lithophane lamps & photo frames

A photograph printed as a graded panel — flat and pale in daylight, then astonishing when it is backlit. Made as table lamps, night lights, heart and arch frames, keyrings and moon lamps. The single most given gift that leaves this workshop, and the one people photograph and send back to us.

Everyday

Keychains, name tags & badges

Names, initials, logos, dates, registration numbers, QR codes that actually scan. Single pieces for a gift, or five hundred for a conference. Two-colour printing means the text is a different colour to the body without a drop of paint.
