Services

Five ways businesses use us

Corporate orders, end-to-end product development, contract manufacturing, bulk orders and OEM. Same workshop, same people, different commercial shape.

01  ·  Corporate orders

Gifting your team will not quietly leave behind

Branded pieces made for your company rather than bought with your logo stuck on afterwards.

Onboarding kits, milestone awards, Diwali and new-year gifting, conference giveaways, desk pieces, trophies, dealer recognition, client hampers. We work from your logo files and brand palette, produce a sample for sign-off, then run the quantity to a committed date.

Typical run: 25 to 2,000 pieces. Lead time: 2 to 4 weeks including sample approval. Comes with: individual polybagging, custom inserts and boxes, name personalisation per piece if you want it.

Brief us on a gifting run

Personalised per recipient

Every piece can carry a different name, date or employee ID at no extra setup — the machine does not care that piece 340 says something different.

Packed and ready to hand out

Sleeved, boxed, labelled and sorted by branch or department, so nobody spends a Saturday packing them.

Designed, not just printed

If you have a concept, we refine it. If you have only a logo, we design around it and show you three directions.

Dated, not estimated

Corporate gifting has a date attached to it. Yours gets a committed dispatch date in writing on the quotation.

02  ·  End-to-end product development

From a sketch on the back of a bill to something you can sell

The whole path, with one team accountable for it — so nothing gets lost between the designer, the printer and the packer.

Most people who come to us with a product idea have a clear picture in their head and no drawing. We turn that into CAD, print a first physical prototype, put it in your hands, and change it until it is right. Then we produce it — without a mould, so the tenth revision costs no more than the first.

Where this saves real money: tooling for injection moulding runs into lakhs and locks the design. Printed production lets you launch, sell a few hundred, learn what is wrong, and fix it before committing to a mould — if you ever need one at all.

03  ·  Contract manufacturing

Your design, our machines, your schedule

For companies who already have the drawing and need it made, repeatedly, without buying a printer farm and hiring someone to run it.

You send the file and the specification. We agree the material, tolerance, finish and inspection standard once, freeze it as a control document, and every batch after that is measured against it. Reorder by email; the spec does not drift.

  •  Fixed spec sheet agreed before the first batch
  •  Dimensional inspection against your drawing, batch by batch
  •  Held stock and call-off schedules for regular volumes
  •  GST invoicing, delivery challans and documentation your accounts team expects

Repeatability

The same printer profile, the same filament batch where possible, the same operator checklist. Batch 9 matches batch 1.

Your IP stays yours

NDA first. Your files are not reused, not shown, and not printed for anyone else.

Call-off delivery

Produce once, deliver in instalments against your schedule instead of filling your store room.

Costed openly

Material, machine time and finishing are itemised so you can see exactly what a design change would save.

04  ·  Bulk orders

Per-unit prices that fall as the quantity rises

Hundreds to thousands of identical pieces, printed in parallel across the farm and delivered on a schedule you set.

Volume changes the economics in your favour: setup is amortised, machines run continuously, and material is bought in for the job. The quotation shows every price break so you can decide whether stretching from 400 to 500 pieces is worth it — usually it is.
