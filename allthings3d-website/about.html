About us

Because your ideas deserve to exist

All Things 3D is a custom 3D printing workshop in Mumbai. We started by making the things people could not buy, and grew into the workshop that makes them at quantity for other businesses.

Our story

It started with things that did not exist yet

Almost every first order we take is something you cannot buy. A photograph of a grandmother, turned into a lamp. A knob that snapped off a machine nobody makes parts for any more. A trophy with a specific person's name on it. A prototype somebody needed to hold before they could explain it to an investor.

What we noticed is that those orders keep coming back bigger. The person who ordered one keychain for a friend comes back in March asking for four hundred for their company. The founder who wanted one prototype now needs a thousand units a quarter. So the workshop grew in that direction — same care on a single keepsake, but with the process, documentation and capacity to run production behind it.

That is the whole business, really: take the idea seriously, whatever size it arrives in.

How we work

Four things we do not compromise on

01

We answer quickly

One working day, every enquiry, with a real answer rather than an acknowledgement.

02

We say when it will not work

Before you pay. A thin wall that will crack in transit, a material that will warp in a car — you hear it early.

03

We check before we ship

Measured against the drawing, fit-tested where there is a mating part, photographed before it is packed.

04

We put it right

A piece that does not match what was approved is remade at our cost. No argument, no invoice.

Who we work with

Four kinds of people send us files

People with something to give

A photograph that should be a lamp, a name that should be on a keyring, a moment that should be an object. Usually one piece. Often the start of a much bigger order.

Founders with a product

An idea that needs to become a thing that can be held, tested, photographed and sold — without spending a fortune on tooling to find out whether it works.

Companies who need it made

Manufacturers, engineering firms and design studios who have the drawing and need consistent batches, on schedule, to a written spec.

Brands who need a workshop

Retailers and gifting companies selling under their own name, with production, packing and dispatch handled quietly behind them.

The name

Why “All Things”

Because the honest answer to “can you print this?” is almost always yes, and the interesting part of the conversation is everything that comes after — which material, what finish, how thick that wall needs to be, and whether it should be one piece or four.

The tagline underneath the logo is not decoration. Because your ideas deserve to exist is a statement about what a workshop is for. Most good ideas die as descriptions. Ours is the job of making them into objects.
