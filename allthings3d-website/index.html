For business

Manufacturing without the tooling bill

Injection moulding makes sense at fifty thousand pieces. Below that, the mould costs more than the parts. 3D printing skips the tooling entirely — so a design change on Tuesday is in production on Wednesday, and a run of two hundred is genuinely viable.

  •  No mould cost, no minimum of ten thousand
  •  Design revisions between batches at no tooling penalty
  •  NDA signed before your files ever open
  •  Consistent spec, batch after batch, with dimensional checks
